# Financial Analysis Metric Test Questions

This document contains sample questions grouped by metric category for validating financial analysis metrics.

## `analysis_income_yearly_growth`

1. How did TCS's net sales grow year over year over the last 5 years?
2. What is the YoY revenue growth of Infosys from FY22 to FY26?
3. Show me gross sales year-on-year change for Reliance Industries over the past 4 years.
4. How has HCL Technologies' net sales changed annually since FY21?
5. Compare year-over-year income growth for Wipro over the last 3 years.

## `analysis_expenditure_yearly_growth`

1. How did TCS's employee costs change year over year over the last 4 years?
2. What is the YoY growth in raw material costs for Reliance Industries since FY22?
3. Show annual change in operating expenses for Infosys from FY21 to FY26.
4. How has power and fuel cost grown year over year for Tata Steel over the last 5 years?
5. What is the year-on-year change in finance charges for HDFC Bank over the past 3 years?

## `analysis_derived_yearly_growth`

1. How did TCS's EBITDA grow year over year over the last 5 years?
2. What is the annual growth rate of net profit for Infosys from FY22 to FY26?
3. Show me year-on-year PAT change for Reliance Industries over the last 4 years.
4. How has operating profit grown annually for Wipro since FY21?
5. What is the YoY growth in EBITDA margin for HCL Technologies over the last 3 years?
